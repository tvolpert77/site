Southern Voice Branding

April 2023

As apart of a desktop publishing class I took at NC State, one of our projects was to find a client and do work for them using Photoshop, Illustrator, and Indesign from the Adobe suite.

I decided to use my mother as my client. Her country band logo was previously just word art and she was looking for a new one.

I additionally designed a business card (front and back) for her. This contains all of her contact information she wished to have, as well as a QR code that brings you to her website.
