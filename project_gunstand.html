Firearm Display Stands

November 2025

A local firearm shop (shoutout to Lock N' Load) had asked if I could make a custom stand for the Glock 43x and the Sig Sauer P365 Macro, both with their respective logos on the stand. They actually got two of each because I messed up the tolerances the first time around, but the stands work for other models like the Glock 19 and the Sig P320 (have to be careful around the P320 though).

This was a quick and easy make. I am more proud of myself for using this to barter for a free Massachusetts License-To-Carry class, but I also felt like sharing this to show that I can do quick and easy requests like this.
