The Player's Club Playground Model

November 2024

In my "Research & Development in Technology Education" class at NC State, all students were tasked with designing a playground that has at least three features (while also promoting accessibility).

I have ZERO interest in playground design. I was also really enjoying using my 3D printer. I was going to use this as an opportunity to have a little bit of fun.

Behold, my 100% 3D printed casino themed playground (consider looking at the documentation linked below).
